## Corrections already applied

**`liu2022` was cited for an empirical result it cannot contain.** The entry is Liu and
Zhou, *Introduction to Graph Neural Networks*, a Springer textbook, and it had been cited as
the source of "Liu et al. combined graph attention with a knowledge graph and reported
accuracy gains." That claim came from the superseded manuscript. Both the claim and its row
in Table 1 have been removed.

**Six performance figures were removed as unverifiable**, all inherited from the superseded
manuscript. Two have since been checked and could be reinstated: Rahman et al.'s 914
instances, and PROMISE at 625 requirements, which has been restored to §2.2.

**Two of those figures were wrong, not merely unverified.** Rahimi et al. report 95.7% and
93.4%, not 83.4%. Kurtanović and Maalej report approximately 92% precision and recall, not
F1 = 0.92. Both appeared in earlier drafts of this manuscript.

**`dellanna2023` is indexed as 2022.** Corrected in the bibliography.

## A note on Table 1

The Reported column names each study's metric type rather than its value. This is
deliberate. Values obtained on different corpora under different protocols are not
comparable, and printing them side by side creates the false equivalence the paper argues
against; the caption says so. If you verify a figure and want it reinstated, that is a
defensible choice, but the table's purpose is to show incomparability rather than to invite
comparison.
